Institutional Profile & Mission

A Global Philanthropic Bridge for Vulnerable Riverbank Communities

Ray Chowdhury Foundation (RCF) is a registered humanitarian organization established to transform the lives of underserved families living on the erosion-prone river islands (chars) of Fulchari, Gaibandha.

Guided by our founding motto 'Every Life Deserves a Future', we unite expatriate professionals in North America and Europe with seasoned field teams on the ground in Bangladesh to deliver swift, audited, and dignified community empowerment.
